# Retrieval Augmented Generation - Technology Brief  
## What it is  
Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) enhances large language models (LLMs) by retrieving external knowledge to improve accuracy, reduce hallucinations, and support domain-specific or up-to-date information [6]. It integrates external databases into LLMs to enable contextually relevant, factually grounded responses [8].  

## Maturity  
Estimated TRL 8: RAG systems are widely deployed in commercial products, with strong industry adoption and integration into enterprise search and domain-specific applications. Publication data shows rapid growth and industry involvement, with 22% of top papers including industry authors, indicating real-world use [OpenAlex].

## Trends  
Publication volume has surged from 37 in 2020 to 30,152 in 2026 (partial), reflecting exponential growth in research activity. Innovation is shifting toward graph-based RAG (e.g., GRAG, GraphRAG) to better handle structured, networked data [5][10]. Efficiency improvements (e.g., LightRAG) and automated evaluation (e.g., RAGas) are emerging to address scalability and accuracy challenges [3][8][9].  

## Applications and open challenges  
Applications span medicine, enterprise search, and knowledge-intensive domains where factual accuracy is critical [2][4]. Challenges remain in retrieval quality evaluation, contextual awareness, and integration of complex interdependencies in data [9][8]. While RAG reduces hallucinations by grounding answers in retrieved documents [6], performance bottlenecks across LLMs and retrieval methods persist [7].
